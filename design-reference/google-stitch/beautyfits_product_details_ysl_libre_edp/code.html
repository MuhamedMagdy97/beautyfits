Product Specifications & Commitments

Yves Saint Laurent Libre Eau de Parfum represents a daring statement of freedom. The structured rigidity of masculine lavender is subverted and softened by ultra-feminine white florals, creating an audacious tension between fierce determination and magnetic sensuality.

The flacon itself reflects couture architecture: geometric lines inspired by Saint Laurent's tailored tuxedos, draped in the iconic gold Cassandre monogram pinned like an haute-couture brooch, crowned with an asymmetric jet-black lacquered cap wrapped in gold chains.

Longevity 8 to 12 Hours Excellent hold even in warm Egyptian climates.
Sillage / Projection Moderate to Heavy Leaves a distinct, magnetic trail without overwhelming.
Ideal Occasions Day-to-Night Signature Boardrooms, evening soirees, and special galas.

Application Ritual: Mist onto pulse points: wrists, collarbone, behind ears, and inside elbows. Avoid rubbing wrists together after application, as this breaks down top aromatic molecular clusters.

Country of Origin: Made in France
