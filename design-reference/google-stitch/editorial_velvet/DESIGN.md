## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a luxurious, editorial beauty storefront tailored specifically for the modern Middle Eastern consumer. It balances European prestige apothecary aesthetics with high-velocity transactional clarity. The interface speaks to aspirational elegance, trust, sensory indulgence, and refined exclusivity. 

Visually, the system marries **Modern Editorial Luxury** with **Tactile Minimalism**. It relies on generous breathing room, crisp typographical hierarchies, whisper-quiet structural lines, and physical cues that mirror high-end cosmetic packaging: frosted glass bottles, embossed textured card stocks, and polished metallic touches. Interactions must feel deliberate, weightless, and velvety—never flashy, clunky, or transactional.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in vintage cosmetic heritage and warm, radiant skin tones.

- **Primary (`#6B1E3F` - Deep Burgundy):** Carries authority, heritage, and depth. Applied to critical callouts, primary buttons, editorial headlines, and the universal announcement bar.
- **Secondary (`#C98A9B` - Soft Rose):** Acts as a romantic, approachable companion tone. Used for subtle hover states, badge accents, and gentle decorative accents.
- **Tertiary (`#C9A46C` - Champagne Gold):** A precious luxury accent used sparingly—strictly reserved for VIP tiers, star ratings, specialty curation stamps, and promotional highlights.
- **Neutral (`#242124` - Charcoal):** Replaces pure black to maintain an organic, ink-on-vellum editorial aesthetic. Secondary body uses Muted Mauve Gray (`#756B70`) for soft contrast.
- **Surfaces & Borders:** Canvas sits on Warm Ivory (`#FFF9F5`) and Soft Cream (`#F6EEE8`), giving digital surfaces a warm, non-sterile paper feel. Interactive containers reside on Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) with delicate borders (`#E8DDE0`).
- **Semantic Feedback:** Success / In-Stock (`#477A5B`), Sale / Urgency (`#B83A52`), and Pending Status (`#C99A46`) communicate logistics without breaking visual continuity.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm depends on the juxtaposition between the romantic high-fashion editorial serif (`Playfair Display`) and the clean, structural geometric sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).

- **Playfair Display:** Exclusively for editorial narrative, section heroes, campaign banners, and high-level headings. It should never be tracked out excessively; keep tracking natural or slightly tight to preserve letterform elegance.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Drives transaction, conversion, and operational clarity. It governs navigation, body copy, technical ingredient lists, UI controls, and prices.
- **EGP Pricing Convention:** Price figures use `Plus Jakarta Sans` with bold weights (`700`). The currency symbol ("EGP") is set in `label-sm` or `label-md` with `0.06em` letter spacing, placed either adjacent or superscribed to provide clarity without interrupting the visual scan.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a 12-column responsive fluid grid pinned to a maximum container width of `1360px` for ultra-wide displays to maintain an editorial feel without extreme stretching.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile (`< 768px`): 4 columns, `margin-sm` (1.25rem / 20px), `gutter-sm` (1rem / 16px).
  - Tablet (`768px – 1024px`): 8 columns, 2rem margins, 1.25rem gutters.
  - Desktop (`> 1024px`): 12 columns, `margin` (3rem / 48px), `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px).
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Large sections maintain expansive breathing room (`space-xl` to `6rem`) between catalog modules, product carousels, and visual breaks, evoking editorial spreads rather than crowded discount catalogs.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is rendered subtly to preserve a flat, tactile editorial feel reminiscent of fine stationery.

- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** Primary visual separation uses `1px` solid `#E8DDE0`. Borders define cards, filters, and dropdown containers rather than harsh shadows.
- **Ambient Diffused Shadows:** For floating cart drawers, interactive popovers, and sticky headers, use soft, ultra-low opacity tinted drops:
  - Floating Panels: `0px 12px 32px rgba(107, 30, 63, 0.06), 0px 2px 6px rgba(36, 33, 36, 0.03)`
  - Hover Elevation on Product Cards: Instead of sharp lift, cards subtly bloom with `0px 8px 24px rgba(107, 30, 63, 0.08)` while the border transitions smoothly from `#E8DDE0` to `#C98A9B`.
- **Tonal Layers:** Layered hierarchy is established with soft canvas stepping: Warm Ivory (`#FFF9F5`) as the base ground, Soft Cream (`#F6EEE8`) for secondary bands or modules, and pure `#FFFFFF` for interactive cards.

## Shapes
The shape language aligns with architectural elegance and bespoke luxury cosmetics packaging. Softened structural radii (Level 1) prevent elements from feeling cold or aggressive, while deliberately avoiding the overly playful, bubble-like appearance of hyper-rounded corners.

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to badges, form inputs, mini tags, and subtle image frames.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to modal sheets, cart drawers, customer account panels, and primary interactive cards.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for interactive chip tags, sticky promotional flags, status indicators, and the COD progress indicator bar.

## Components

### Announcement Bar
A fixed 34px ribbon set in Deep Burgundy (`#6B1E3F`) with Crisp Ivory text (`label-sm`). Content rotates smoothly between key customer assurances: COD availability across Cairo/Giza, authentic luxury guarantees, and the **2,500 EGP Free Shipping** threshold.

### Desktop Header
An 82px persistent header over Warm Ivory (`#FFF9F5`) with a delicate lower border (`#E8DDE0`). Centralized navigation uses `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`label-md`) with an understated burgundy underline on active states. Right utility features minimal iconography: Wishlist (with heart indicator counter), Cart Drawer trigger, and Account dropdown.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Aspect ratio `3:4` beauty photography mounted against Soft Cream or crisp studio backgrounds. Enclosed in a 1px `#E8DDE0` frame with `rounded-lg` corners.
- **Badges:** Top-left stacked micro-pills (`label-sm`, all caps, tracking `0.08em`):
  - NEW: Ivory text on `#6B1E3F`.
  - BESTSELLER: Deep Burgundy text on Champagne Gold wash (`#C9A46C` at 15% opacity).
  - SALE: Pure white text on `#B83A52`.
  - OUT OF STOCK: Soft grayed tone on `#756B70`.
- **Wishlist:** Top-right circular floating action button with hover scale and deep burgundy fill when active.
- **Metadata:** Brand name in `label-sm` (`#756B70`), product title in `title-md` truncated to two lines, 5-star rating featuring `#C9A46C` filled stars.
- **Pricing:** Highlighted in `price-md` with `EGP` symbol clearly demarcated. Strikethrough pricing rendered in `#756B70`.
- **Hover Quick Action:** Subtle sliding drawer at card bottom offering an instant "Add to Bag" or shade swatch quick-selector without obscuring the product visual.

### COD (Cash on Delivery) Checkout Progress & Alerts
- Free Shipping milestone tracker: A thin, elegant progress rail (`4px` height, `#E8DDE0` track, `#6B1E3F` fill) showing remaining amount required to unlock free shipping ("Add 450 EGP more for Free Delivery").
- COD Verification module: A dedicated checkout step with a soft beige-tinted container, reassuring copy on contactless payment options, phone verification badges, and cash receipt terms.

### Account Sidebar & Order Badges
- Sidebar features customer name, loyalty tier, and store wallet balance (`#6B1E3F` highlight box).
- Status Badges:
  - *Pending Confirmation:* Soft Cream background with `#C99A46` typography.
  - *Delivered:* Ivory background with `#477A5B` typography.
  - *Returned:* Soft rose wash with `#B83A52` typography.
- Return action button displaying real-time calculation of remaining days in the 14-day statutory return window.

### Buttons & Form Inputs
- **Primary Buttons:** Solid `#6B1E3F`, white text, `rounded-sm` (4px), 48px height for desktop with slight tracking. Active states deepen to a rich wine tone.
- **Secondary Buttons:** Transparent fill, `1.5px` border in `#6B1E3F`, text in Deep Burgundy.
- **Form Inputs:** 46px height, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px` `#E8DDE0`, focused border `#6B1E3F` with no harsh default browser glow. Label floating in `label-sm`.
